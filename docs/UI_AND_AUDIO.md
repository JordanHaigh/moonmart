# UI, Typography, Audio and Accessibility

## UI
Retain concept-art rounded friendly handwritten lettering as reference, not an identified font. Create a custom Moonmart logo; license a real heading and body font before shipping. HUD: time/date, coins/tokens, minimal objective, contextual interaction prompt. Journal, ledger, map, inventory, supplier catalogue, recipe book and decoration editor appear as layered parchment/lavender panels. Avoid oversized visual-novel dialogue covering characters. Use in-world speech bubbles for casual dialogue and a compact bottom panel for longer conversations.

## Audio
Original piano, warm synth, soft guitar, music box and brushed percussion. Rain on windows, fridge hum, clock ticks, cup clinks, cat purr and muted street sounds. During anomalies, alter ambience subtly rather than jump scares. Dynamic music by location, weather and story state.

## Accessibility
Fully remappable input; controller; UI scale; readable body type; reduced motion/flashing; dialogue auto-advance toggle; assisted checkout; simplified crafting; adjustable time speed; pause during menus and dialogue; three mystery hint levels; save anywhere.
