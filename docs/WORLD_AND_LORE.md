# Bellwether & The Between — Lore Bible

Bellwether is a sleepy forest-and-coast town. By day ordinary; at night the boundary with the Between thins. The Between contains lost places, memories, dreams and travelers. It is neither afterlife nor hell.

## Rules
Names and promises carry power. Forgotten memories may materialize as objects. Doors connect impossible distances. Time may fold. People forgotten by everyone risk slipping away. Keep some uncertainty; not all phenomena need exhaustive exposition.

## The Long Midnight
Decades ago sunrise did not come for an entire night. People disappeared, streets changed and strangers arrived. Most locals forgot. Moonmart stands over a doorway between realms. An old agreement made the shop a safe rest stop for travelers as long as it stayed open at night. The agreement is failing; people and places begin disappearing from memory.

## Districts
Old Bellwether: bakery, bookshop, antique shop, post office.
Whisperwood: shifting forest paths, glowing flora, forgotten shrine.
Moonwater Harbour: fishing village, impossible objects and night boats.
Old Lighthouse: strange beam guiding travelers.
Midnight Platform: station not on maps, trains between realms.
Moonmart: evolving sanctuary with retail floor, backroom, tea corner, reading nook, greenhouse and hidden room.

## Mystery themes
Who gets remembered? What makes a place home? Is moving on always necessary? Can worlds coexist without one consuming the other? Player decisions should be bittersweet, never cruel for shock value.
