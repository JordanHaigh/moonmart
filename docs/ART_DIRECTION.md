# Moonmart — Art Direction

## Canonical references
The original supplied illustration (sleeping orange-and-cream cat, glowing nightlight, ivy, moonlit shop window) is the primary art-style authority. The subsequent wide Moonmart gameplay mockup establishes the spatial composition and gameplay HUD.

## Locked visual language
- Hand-painted 2D, **not pixel art**.
- Deep indigo and lavender shadows; warm peach, rose, and cream light.
- Soft brush textures, rounded silhouettes, understated outlines, delicate foliage.
- Side-on environments with shallow walkable depth and parallax exterior views.
- Rounded, friendly handwritten logo and headings; accessible rounded body typeface.
- No third-party font files are included; select a suitably licensed font.

## Asset categories
Architecture: walls, floors, windows, doors, arches, trim.
Furniture: shelving, refrigerator, checkout, drink station, displays, storage.
Characters: player, Miso, Pip, Agnes, Fern, Lucien, Wren, Conductor.
Products: groceries, milk, pastries, teas, enchanted items.
Atmosphere: vines, potted flowers, star lamps, moonlight, steam, ghost glow.
Interface: clock, currency, journal, objectives, contextual prompts.

## Production requirements
Deliver separate transparent sprites, layered backgrounds, consistent character pivots and collision footprints. Use reference-resolution 1920×1080. Keep high-resolution editable sources. Avoid baking characters, interactive furniture, or UI into background paintings.
