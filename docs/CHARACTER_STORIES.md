# Moonmart — Character Narrative Notes

## Miso
Introduced sleeping on counter. Reacts to ghosts before player understands the Between. Gradually unlocks Spirit Sense, Hidden Paw, Dreamwalking, Memory Echo, Guardian's Purr. Reveal: has guarded the shop longer than an ordinary cat can live. Avoid cat harm as emotional leverage.

## Pip
Visits Tuesdays for strawberry milk. First clue: no reflection. Second clue: receipt dated 1974. Third: old photograph. Their story concerns recovering memories without invalidating the family they found in the present. Arc ends with an emotionally gentle choice about belonging.

## Agnes Thorne
Baker across the street, warm and practical. Brings leftover pastries. Knows about the Long Midnight and once helped maintain the boundary. Reveals knowledge slowly as player earns trust.

## Fern
Shy forest spirit and gardening customer. Whisperwood loses birdsong as memories fade. Player restores grove by finding lost seeds, resolving a promise and planting a moonflower.

## Lucien Vale
Tired vampire librarian, decaf coffee and detective novels. Main arc about friendship with a human growing older; player helps them speak honestly rather than trying to solve aging.

## Wren Hollow
Travelling witch and magical stock supplier. Searches for a passenger who boarded the Midnight Train and never returned. Unlocks magical kettle, enchanted inventory and clues to the platform.

## The Conductor
Appears at farewells and station events. Keeps records of forgotten travelers. Knows why Moonmart is a sanctuary; not an antagonist.

## Elias Marsh
Last lighthouse keeper. Lighthouse machinery was removed yet light occasionally shines. Its real purpose is guiding lost travelers, not ships.

## Relationship model
Stranger → Familiar → Friend → Confidant → Kindred. Meaningful choices, remembered preferences, shared scenes and small acts of care. No mandatory gift grinding. Optional romances not in v1 scope.
