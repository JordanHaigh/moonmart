# First Five Nights — Vertical Slice

**Night 1, Monday:** Open the store, learn movement, stock three products, serve one customer, meet Agnes and Miso. End with a warm closing summary.

**Night 2, Tuesday:** Pip arrives requesting strawberry milk. A window reveals no reflection. Unlock the customer journal.

**Night 3, Wednesday:** A receipt dated 1974 appears in the register with Pip's order. Unlock the first clue.

**Night 4, Thursday:** Inspect an old photograph, speak with Agnes, connect evidence in the mystery board.

**Night 5, Friday:** Help Pip recover a first memory. Miso leads the player to a previously hidden door behind the shop.

## Vertical slice acceptance
Player movement and collisions; interactive shelves, register and doors; one completed sale; one NPC browsing independently; persistent inventory and quest flags; clock and shift summary; journal and one solvable deduction; save/load; controller-compatible actions; Miso and Pip animation prototypes.
